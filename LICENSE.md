# Fluxpoint licensing

Fluxpoint is multi-licensed by artifact type:

| Artifact | License | Full text |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware (schematics, PCB, case, fabrication files) | CERN-OHL-S-2.0 | [LICENSE/CERN-OHL-S-2.0.txt](LICENSE/CERN-OHL-S-2.0.txt) |
| Firmware and software | MIT | [LICENSE/MIT.txt](LICENSE/MIT.txt) |
| Documentation | CC BY 4.0 | [LICENSE/CC-BY-4.0.txt](LICENSE/CC-BY-4.0.txt) |

See [NOTICE](NOTICE) for third-party attribution, including the Corne keyboard.

CERN-OHL-S-2.0 is strongly reciprocal: derivative hardware must be licensed under the same terms. Corne-derived files retain their original MIT/CC BY 4.0 notices; do not relicense them.
