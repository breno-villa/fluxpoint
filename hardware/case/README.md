# Case and plates

Planned Phase 2 deliverables: a 3D-printable case and FR4 switch plate for each half.

## Requirements

- 42-key Choc V1 layout, 18 x 17 mm spacing.
- Target height 15–16 mm with MBK keycaps.
- Battery pocket under the controller for a 502030 cell (5 x 20 x 30 mm, plus protection/wires).
- 4x N52 magnet pockets in the bottom (8x2 or 10x2 mm; final size after pull test).
- Flat seating area and rubber feet for desk use.
- Access to USB-C, reset, power switch, OLED, TrackPoint cap, and battery connector.
- TrackPoint bracket: two ears/holes at ~12.8 mm, bottom sensor grid clear, flex strain relief.
- Antenna keepout around the controller; no battery/magnet directly over the antenna.
- Controller socket at the top edge, below keycap height.

## Print settings

- PETG or ABS; 0.2 mm layers; 3–4 perimeters; 20–30% infill.
- Print a fit coupon for the switch plate, magnet pockets, and TrackPoint bracket before the full case.
- Use heat-set inserts or self-tapping screws for serviceable assembly.
